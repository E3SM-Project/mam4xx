# Theory Guide

The Theory Guide explains the physical processes represented in MAM4xx and
connects their equations and assumptions to the implementation. It is intended
for scientists and developers who need to understand a process before working
through its source code.

The first chapter is [Sulfur chemistry](sulfur-chemistry.md). It follows sulfur
from gas precursors to sulfate, explains the roles of oxidants, sunlight, and
cloud water, and describes the numerical coupling to aerosol microphysics.
Its source map and review record identify the inspected versions and remaining
scientific questions.

This guide describes specific source snapshots. Each chapter records its own
evidence and review status; source inspection and a successful documentation
build do not establish numerical validation.
